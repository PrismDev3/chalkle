WELCOME TO LUNCHBREAK
✕
Welcome!
Lunchbreak is a retro-PC style chat where you can create rooms, meet people, and message friends.
Tip: You can bookmark this page so you can come back anytime!
Rooms
Join public rooms to chat with everyone, or create your own private room and invite friends with a room code. Hosts can kick, mute, and @everyone.
Tip: Use !code=ROOMCODE in a message to invite someone to a room!
Direct Messages
Send private messages to anyone. Click their avatar to open their profile, then hit the DM button. Your inbox lives in the left panel under the INBOX tab.
Tip: You can paste images directly into any chat with Ctrl+V!
Friends
Add friends to keep track of people you like chatting with. Click someone's avatar → Add Friend. Accept requests in the REQUESTS tab.
Tip: Friends show up in your list on the left side: green dot means they're online, flashing dot means they're in a room
Customize
Open Settings from the top menu to pick a color theme, adjust display size, and control notifications. Make it yours!
Tip: Try the Latte or Midnight themes for a different vibe
